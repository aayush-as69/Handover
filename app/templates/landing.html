Every claim gets one of four verdicts

Computed by the checker, never typed in by hand.

quote-verified
The quotes exist in the sources and fit the claim. That is not the same as true; the reviewer still decides.
inferred
The quote exists, but the claim adds a number, a date or a conclusion it does not contain.
conflicting
A quote in the sources contradicts the claim.
unsupported
None of the cited quotes can be found in the sources.

Three things Handover will not do

  1. Publish without a person. Nothing is approved automatically, not even a quote-verified claim.
  2. Show what your role may not see. A claim is as sensitive as its most sensitive source, and secrets are masked on the way in.
  3. Change a published release. Releases are numbered and fixed. Corrections go into the next one.
