A clear handover process

From scattered information
to shared understanding.

One practical workflow keeps the source, the claim and the decision connected.

01

Collect the context

Bring in notes, conversations, task exports and public repository activity. Secrets are masked during ingestion.

02

Check every claim

Extract decisions, owners, risks and dependencies. Link each claim to source evidence and flag gaps or contradictions.

03

Review and hand over

A person approves the claims. Approved versions become numbered releases with role-based visibility.

Inside the product

Not just a summary.
A trail you can inspect.

Reviewers see the claim, the exact quote behind it, why it received its label and what changed over time. They can approve, edit, reject or reopen it.

  • Riskiest claims appear first.
  • Every evidence quote links to its source.
  • Edits are checked again and require approval.

Evidence, made understandable

Four labels. No hidden confidence score.

The checker explains what it found. These labels are evidence checks—not guarantees that a statement is true.

quote-verified

The quote exists and passes the mechanical support checks.

inferred

The quote exists, but the claim goes beyond what it supports.

conflicting

Source evidence contradicts the claim.

unsupported

No usable supporting quote was found, or no evidence was attached.

Designed around human judgment

AI can help prepare the handover.
It cannot approve it.

Automation helps organize project knowledge without becoming the authority on what gets shared.

01

AI is optional

Rule-based extraction works without an API key. Model output is validated and remains a draft.

02

Access is enforced

Role and source sensitivity determine what each reader can see. Confidential chunks are withheld from remote extraction by default.

03

Releases keep history

Published releases are numbered and fixed. Corrections are reviewed and published in a later release.
