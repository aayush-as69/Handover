Sources come in. Secrets don’t stay.

Handover reads notes, chat exports, task lists and public GitHub activity. Before anything is stored, passwords, keys and tokens are masked. A chunk that needed masking is marked confidential from then on.

team_chat.txt

riya: seats keep double booking, I rotated the staging key, api_key=sk-test-91f3c07a22, don’t paste it anywhere

This is what was in the export.

riya: seats keep double booking, I rotated the staging key, api_key=[REDACTED:secret], don’t paste it anywhere

This is all Handover keeps. The chunk is now confidential, and confidential chunks are withheld from remote models by default.

Claims are drafted, each with its quote.

Handover pulls out decisions, owners, risks, dependencies and open questions. Each draft claim carries the exact words it was taken from, and a link back to the source.

Every claim gets one of four labels.

The checker compares each claim with its quote and says what it found. There is no hidden score. A label is an evidence check, not a promise that the claim is true.

A person decides. Always.

Handover never approves anything. A reviewer can approve a claim, edit it, reject it or reopen it later. An edited claim is checked again and needs approval again.

Each reader sees their share.

Every claim has a sensitivity, and every role has a clearance. A reader below the clearance doesn’t get a blurred version. They get nothing, or a version with the private detail removed.

A mentor sees team-level claims only.

Releases keep their history.

Approved claims are published as numbered releases. A published release never changes. A correction is reviewed and goes out in the next one, with the difference shown.

Release 1 stays exactly as it was published. The next release shows what changed.
